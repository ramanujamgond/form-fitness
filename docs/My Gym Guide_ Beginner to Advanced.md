# My Gym Guide

Built from the machines in your photos. Pick your level and every machine card changes its instructions. Move up a level only after you finish 2 weeks of clean reps at the top of the rep range.

Showing Beginner: light weights, RIR 3, learn the posture first.

## Learn the 4 words first

### Rep

One full movement, for example one pull-down from top to bottom and back.

### Set

A group of reps done without a break. 3 × 10 means 10 reps, rest, 10 reps, rest, 10 reps. It is not 30 in a row.

### RIR (reps in reserve)

How many good reps you could still do. RIR 2 means about 2 more. Most sets should end at RIR 1–3, not at failure.

### Double progression

Aim for 3 × 8–12. When all 3 sets reach 12 with good form, add a small weight. Then restart near 8.

## How to find your starting weight (first session on a new machine)

**1**

Pick the lightest weight. Do 10 slow reps with the posture below.

**2**

Ask yourself: could I do 3 or more extra reps? If yes, rest 1 minute and go up one step on the stack (or add one small plate per side).

**3**

Repeat. Stop when rep 10 feels hard but your posture stays perfect and you could do about 2 more. That is your weight. It usually takes 2–4 tries.

**4**

Write it down with the machine name. Next session, start there.

If you can't keep the posture, the weight is too heavy no matter what the number says.

## Posture checkpoints (side view)

These are simple drawings, not photos. Use them as a checklist, then compare with yourself in the gym mirror.

### Lat pulldown

1. Thigh pad snug over your thighs
2. Feet flat, chest up, small lean back
3. Elbows travel down, bar to upper chest
4. Don't swing or shrug

### T-bar row (chest supported)

1. Chest stays on the pad all the time
2. Feet flat on the platform
3. Pull elbows back past your ribs
4. Squeeze shoulder blades, then lower slowly

### Incline chest press

1. Back and head rest on the pad
2. Shoulder blades pulled back and down
3. Handles near mid-chest, feet flat
4. Press up, don't lock elbows hard

## Get a real person to check you (do this in week 1)

- **Film yourself from the side.** Prop your phone at hip height, 3 metres away, record one set of 10 reps.

## Level ladder

**Beginner (months 0–3)**\
Learn form. Light weights, RIR 3, 3 sets per exercise. Goal: no missed form, steady 4 days a week.

**Intermediate (months 3–8)**\
Add weight by double progression. 4 sets on main lifts. Slower lowering. Track every session.

**Advanced (months 8+)**\
Heavy top sets plus back-off sets, pauses, drop sets on the last set only. Take a lighter week every 6–8 weeks.

## Progress rule in one picture

**1**

Target 3 × 8–12. Start with a weight at RIR 2–3.

**2**

Week by week: 10/9/8, then 11/10/9, then 12/11/10, then 12/12/12.

**3**

12/12/12 with good form: add a small weight, expect 8/8/8, then build again.

**4**

If reps fall like 12/9/7 and form breaks, the weight is too heavy. If you hit 15/15/15 easily, it is too light.

**Stop and get checked if you feel:** sharp pain, joint pain, sudden pain, numbness, tingling, dizziness, chest pain, or unusual breathlessness. Muscle fatigue and normal soreness are fine. Never push through sharp pain.

Abs show when body fat drops. Do 2–3 core sessions a week (cable crunch, knee raise, plank) and keep your protein around 120–150 g a day.
