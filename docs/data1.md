# One thing to change right away: warm-up

Your 100 push-ups and 90 squats are not a warm-up. A warm-up prepares you; working sets create the training effect. If you spend your energy on 100 push-ups first, your real chest work gets weaker. From now on, use this:

1. 5–8 min brisk walk, bike, or elliptical
2. Shoulder circles, hip and ankle mobility
3. 1–3 light ramp-up sets of your first exercise
4. Push-ups and squats become scheduled exercises, not a warm-up
